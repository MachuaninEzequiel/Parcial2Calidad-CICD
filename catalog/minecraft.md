The sandbox phenomenon: an open world made entirely of blocks, generated procedurally so no two worlds are alike. You mine resources by hand, gather wood, stone, ore and more, then craft them into tools, weapons and building materials to shape the landscape however you imagine. In Survival mode you manage hunger and health, dig deep for diamonds, and fend off skeletons, creepers and zombies that come out at night, building a shelter to make it to dawn. In Creative mode you have infinite materials and free flight to construct anything, from a tiny cabin to a sprawling castle or a working redstone machine. You can play alone and lose hours to your own projects, or join friends on a shared server to build together. With no fixed goals beyond the ones you set yourself, it is pure open-ended creativity, exploration and construction.
