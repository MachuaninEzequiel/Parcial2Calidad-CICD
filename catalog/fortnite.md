The battle royale that turned a hundred players dropping onto one island into a global phenomenon: you land, scavenge for weapons and resources, and fight to be the last one standing as a deadly storm shrinks the playable map and forces everyone closer together. Its signature twist is building: you harvest wood, brick and metal, then instantly throw up walls, ramps and towers to take cover, gain high ground or push an enemy mid-firefight, turning every gunfight into a frantic construction duel. The third-person shooting is snappy and the matches are fast and unpredictable. On top of the core mode, it runs live in-game events, crossover seasons and a constantly changing map, with crossplay across every platform. Free to play and endlessly evolving, it blends shooting, building and spectacle into something no other shooter quite matches.
