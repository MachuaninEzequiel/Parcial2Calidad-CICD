Hades is a fast-paced action roguelike in which Zagreus, prince of the Underworld, tries again and again to escape the realm of the dead by fighting his way past his father, the god Hades. Every run generates different rooms and enemies, and when you die you return to the start to begin anew, stronger and with new weapons and boons from the gods of Olympus. The combat is quick, fluid and immensely satisfying, and its high difficulty is balanced by constant progression that makes each new attempt addictive. The brilliant twist is how the story advances WITH your deaths: the characters remember, comment and reveal more of the plot every time you come back. Stunning art, a charismatic cast and a near-perfect gameplay loop. Dying has never been this much fun.
