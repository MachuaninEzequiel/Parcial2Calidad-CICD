Celeste is a brutally hard precision platformer about Madeline, a determined young woman climbing a haunted mountain while she battles her anxiety and her deepest fears. Every screen is a short, punishing challenge: pixel-perfect jumps, mid-air dashes and spikes that never forgive a mistake. The difficulty is extreme and you die constantly, but restarts are instant and never feel cruel, so they pull you back to try one more time. Beneath the demanding gameplay lives an intimate, deeply emotional indie story about mental health, self-doubt and acceptance. The soundtrack underscores every feeling, and an assist mode lets anyone reach the summit at their own pace. Hard, heartfelt and profoundly human.
