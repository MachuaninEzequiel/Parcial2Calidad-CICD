Stardew Valley is a relaxing farming simulator where you inherit your grandfather's run-down plot of land and leave your office life behind to start over in the countryside. You plant crops, raise animals, go fishing, explore caves full of monsters and minerals, and cook with the produce of each season. The town is full of neighbors with stories of their own: you can befriend them, fall in love, get married and even start a family. There is no pressure and no clock rushing you; you set the pace, and every day offers a thousand things to do however you like. The freedom, the charming pixel art and the gentle sense of slow progress turned it into a beloved phenomenon adored by millions. It is the perfect cozy escape to unwind and build, in your own way, a simpler and calmer life.
