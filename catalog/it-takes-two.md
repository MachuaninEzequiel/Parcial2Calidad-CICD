title: It Takes Two
year: 2021
genres: [adventure, platformer, puzzle]
platforms: [pc, ps, xbox, switch]
---

A co-op adventure built so tightly around two players that there is no single-player mode at all: you must play it together, in split-screen or online, each controlling one half of the story. A bickering couple on the verge of divorce is magically shrunk into a pair of dolls and must work as a team to find their way back. Every level reinvents itself around a brand-new shared mechanic, so the two of you are constantly given different tools that only work in tandem: one swings a hammer while the other dodges, one freezes time while the other moves, one pulls a lever while the other leaps. It mixes platforming, puzzle-solving and inventive set-pieces that demand real communication and coordination, never letting either player coast. Charming, endlessly creative and impossible to play alone, this Game of the Year winner is the definitive two-player cooperative experience.
