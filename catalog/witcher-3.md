The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an open-world RPG in which you play as Geralt of Rivia, a professional monster hunter searching for his adopted daughter while a spectral army threatens the continent. The world is enormous and alive: villages, swamps, cities and seas teeming with contracts, creatures and secrets. What sets it apart is that the choices truly matter; many quests have no clearly good option, and their consequences surface hours later, reshaping the fate of entire regions. Combat blends sword, magical signs and potions, but the soul of the game lives in its side stories, crafted with as much care as the main quest. It is vast, mature and narratively rich, setting an impossibly high bar for the genre.
