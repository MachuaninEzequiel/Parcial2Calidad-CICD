Soccer played with rocket-powered cars, and it is every bit as wild as it sounds. Two teams drive boost-fueled vehicles around a walled arena, trying to smash an oversized ball into the opponent's net. The twist is that you can drive up the walls and fly: skilled players launch into the air for acrobatic aerial shots, flip and dodge mid-flight, and chain dribbles on the roof of their car. Managing your boost meter is its own discipline, deciding when to chase speed and when to save it for a save in goal. Matches scale from intense one versus one duels to chaotic three versus three, all online with crossplay between platforms. Easy to start but with a sky-high skill ceiling, it thrives on competitive ranked play, split-second positioning and the satisfying physics of a perfectly timed bicycle-kick goal.
