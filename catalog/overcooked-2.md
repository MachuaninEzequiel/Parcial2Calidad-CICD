The perfect plan to get friends together on the couch: you sit down side by side in front of the TV and the whole point is coordinating by shouting at each other in the same room. Overcooked 2 is local co-op for two, three or four players, built for hanging out as a group in short, hilarious matches. Each person grabs a role — one chops, another fries, another washes the dishes — and everyone scrambles past one another, prepping and serving orders against the clock in absurd kitchens that move, split apart or have rivers cutting the kitchen in two. It is dead easy to pick up and fiendishly hard to master, so it works just as well for people who have never held a controller as for those chasing the perfect score. The humor, the shared chaos and the small team disasters make it one of the best games for laughing together in the same room.
