title: Hollow Knight

Often recommended as an accessible souls-like, a great gateway into the genre for newcomers: it is hard but fair, never cheap, and every death teaches you something instead of punishing you for nothing. It inherits the core of the souls style — combat that demands precision and patience, recovering your currency where you fell, brutal boss duels — but with a gentler curve that keeps you trying until you finally nail it. Underneath, it is an action platformer and metroidvania set in Hallownest, a vast ruined underground kingdom inhabited by insects: you control a small silent knight exploring interconnected caverns, unlocking abilities little by little to open new areas and uncovering the secrets of a forgotten civilization. The melancholic atmosphere, the orchestral soundtrack and the hand-drawn art make it a deeply emotional experience. Sprawling, cryptic and challenging, it rewards curious exploration over step-by-step guidance.
