The definitive competitive tactical first-person shooter, played five versus five online. Two teams take turns as Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists: one side tries to plant the bomb and protect it until detonation, the other races to defuse it or wipe out the enemy. There are no respawns within a round, so every duel matters and a single bullet can end your life. Gunplay is precise and unforgiving, with recoil patterns you learn by heart and headshots that decide fights instantly. Between rounds you manage a tight economy, deciding when to save money or buy rifles, armor and utility like smokes and flashbangs to control sightlines. Built on the Source 2 engine as the successor to CS:GO, it lives on ranked matchmaking and a massive esports scene where teamwork, map control and clutch plays separate the good from the great.
