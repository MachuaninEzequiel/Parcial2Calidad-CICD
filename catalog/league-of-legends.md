The flagship multiplayer online battle arena, where two teams of five champions fight to destroy each other's Nexus. The map has three lanes and a jungle between them, defended by towers you must topple to push forward. You start each match weak and grow stronger by last-hitting minions for gold, buying items and leveling up your abilities, while contesting neutral objectives like dragons and the Baron Nashor that swing momentum. With more than one hundred and sixty champions, each with a unique kit, drafting and counter-picking is a game in itself before the match even starts. Coordination, vision control with wards, and timing teamfights matter as much as mechanical skill. Deeply strategic and endlessly deep, it is fueled by competitive ranked ladders and one of the largest esports scenes in the world.
