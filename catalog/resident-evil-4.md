A landmark survival horror remake that drips with dread. You play as agent Leon Kennedy, sent to a remote, rain-soaked corner of rural Europe to rescue the president's kidnapped daughter from a sinister cult. What you find is a village of hollow-eyed, infected villagers and grotesque, mutated horrors that stalk you through farmhouses, castles and dark labyrinths. The tense over-the-shoulder aiming makes every shot count as you aim for weak points and crowd-control swarms closing in. Survival means scavenging and rationing scarce ammo and healing herbs, managing a grid inventory where every slot matters, and knowing when to fight and when to run. The pacing tightens the screws with sudden ambushes, dread-soaked silences and brutal boss encounters. Atmospheric, frightening and relentlessly intense, it is the genre at its most refined: pure survival horror built on fear, scarcity and pressure.
