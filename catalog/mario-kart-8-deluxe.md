The definitive kart racing party game, where Nintendo's beloved characters pile into go-karts and tear around wonderfully chaotic tracks. Racing here is never just about speed: you drift around corners to charge a turbo boost, ride anti-gravity stretches that flip the track sideways and onto walls and ceilings, and grab item boxes loaded with shells, banana peels, mushrooms and lightning to attack rivals or defend your lead. Up to twelve racers scramble for position, so a comfortable first place can collapse in the final stretch when a blue shell comes flying. It is easy enough for a child to pick up yet deep enough to master the racing line, and the real magic is multiplayer: split-screen on the couch or racing friends online, with battle modes on the side. Bright, frantic and endlessly replayable, it is the go-to game for chaotic fun in a group.
