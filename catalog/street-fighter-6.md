A one-versus-one fighting game and the latest entry in the genre's most iconic series, where two fighters trade punches, kicks and special moves in a martial-arts duel. Players pick a brawler from a roster of world warriors, including classics like Ryu, Ken, Chun-Li and Guile, and clash in best-of-three rounds of precise hand-to-hand combat. Victory comes from spacing, blocking, reading your opponent and stringing together combos and frame-perfect inputs under pressure. The new Drive system gives every fighter a shared meter to spend on parries, reversals and enhanced specials, adding risk and resource management to each exchange. As a fighting game it is built for ranked online matches and the tournament circuit, with a steep execution ceiling that rewards practice in the lab. A single-player World Tour mode rounds it out, but the heart of it is the head-to-head fight: pure reaction, mind games and mechanical mastery between two players throwing down move for move.
