A social deduction party game played online with friends aboard a spaceship. Most players are Crewmates, working through a checklist of small tasks to keep the ship running, while one or more hidden Impostors secretly sabotage systems and quietly murder the crew one by one. When a body is found or an emergency meeting is called, everyone stops to argue: who was where, who is acting suspicious, who is lying. Then the group votes to eject a suspect out the airlock, hoping they guessed right. The whole fun is the bluffing and the table talk: convincing others you are innocent, accusing a real teammate, or as the Impostor playing it cool while you frame someone else. Quick to learn, hilarious in a group voice chat, and built entirely around trust, betrayal and reading other people, it became a phenomenon for playing online with a circle of friends.
