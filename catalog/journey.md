title: Journey
year: 2012
genres: [indie, adventure, narrative]
platforms: [pc, ps]
---

Journey is a short, luminous adventure in which you guide a robed figure on a pilgrimage across an immense desert toward a distant mountain glowing on the horizon. There is no dialogue, no map and no instructions: everything is told through the landscape, the music and the flowing movement of cloth. Along the way you may cross paths with another real, anonymous player, someone you can only communicate with through a wordless song; together you press on without ever knowing who the other is. The shifting dunes, the ancient temples and the orchestral score build an emotional, intimate experience that lasts a little over an hour. It is a short, silent and unforgettable journey about companionship and transcendence.
